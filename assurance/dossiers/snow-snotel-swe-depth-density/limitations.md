- The five point stations do not represent all climates, canopy states,
  topographies, disturbances, or spatial scales where openWEPP may be used.
- The study used multiple forcing products. Absolute snow magnitude is
  forcing-limited, and forcing uncertainty was not propagated into claim-level
  intervals.
- Depth is a station-derived series; bulk density inherits both depth and SWE
  measurement and processing uncertainty.
- Model variants, rubric cells, and thresholds were evaluated retrospectively.
  There was no independent held-out site partition or prospective acceptance
  plan.
- The tracked observation manifest preserves normalized-file hashes but also
  contains unavailable local acquisition paths and null hashes for some raw or
  intermediate assets. A portable end-to-end replay is incomplete.
- Historical numerical scores are retained in the audit artifacts. They are not
  repeated here as an accuracy grade because the rubric supported a selection
  decision rather than a public fitness claim.
- Package-level implementation review does not substitute for external
  hydrologist review. Its lifecycle remains `CANDIDATE`.
