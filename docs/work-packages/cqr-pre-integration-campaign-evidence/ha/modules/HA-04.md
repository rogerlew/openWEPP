## Operand And Consumer Lineage

The projection recomputes aggregate storage from post-root-uptake layer liquid,
residual, and frozen storage, then compares it with R4B reconciled storage. It
selects runtime projected SWE only when the snow-state flag is active. Runoff,
percolation, subsurface loss, ET components, runon, and carry come from their
typed upstream shadows; publication fields remain comparison-only operands.
The existing anti-alias fixture assigns deliberately different publication and
runtime values, while residual-water and frozen-mismatch tests independently
reconstruct or perturb storage operands. The projection state and downstream
operand object are the real direct-frame surfaces consumed by later publication
builders; the characterization does not promote comparison fields or revive a
compatibility edge.
