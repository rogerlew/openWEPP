## Consumer And Conservation Lineage

Static consumer path: `03_executor.rs` calls `laned_active_route_lane` at the
per-lane active routing step, the route writes `DirectLanedActiveDayRouting`
and `erosion_inputs.routed_hydrograph_runoff_fraction`, and `erosion.rs`
`r7d8_routed_hydrograph_hourly_weights` consumes that frame surface before the
Wave-1 solve. The old DC01 surface-feed path is not a fallback for an active
lane: the executor suppresses its surface admission and
`laned_active_assert_no_dc01_surface_feed` hard-fails any material double feed.

The relevant water operands are source-bin depth rates and upstream unit-flow
handoff on the lane-width basis, solver outlet-bin volumes, mesh end-window
storage, positivity-clamp booking, subsurface/baseflow bypass, and the 24-slot
routed erosion weights. Existing tests independently distinguish outlet-bin
tail mass from in-window mass, zero-local-runoff from routed source, and the
full-mesh-hold source-shape class; `day_closure_enforces_cascade_and_identity_tolerances`
perturbs the separate cascade, clamp, seam, and assembled-identity operands.
Thus the evidence does not merely restate one producer formula, and the
extraction does not touch any operand or closure calculation.
