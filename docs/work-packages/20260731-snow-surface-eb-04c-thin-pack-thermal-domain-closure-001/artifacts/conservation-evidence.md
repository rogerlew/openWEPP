# Conservation Evidence

Evidence class: `Static + Ran`

The total-boundary tests independently compare state before and after a fully
suspended day. They prove unchanged represented SWE and layer cold content and
exactly zero shortwave, longwave, latent, conduction, surface-energy,
sublimation, and routed-melt outputs. A deep low-density vector compares against
an energy-disabled twin and proves the branch occurs before Stage 3 partition
without confusing authoritative CoE compaction with Stage 3 mutation.

The existing Stage 3 whole-pack energy and liquid closures remain in the common
post-loop path. Above the boundary, the pre-existing carrier, conduction,
latent-mass identity, refreeze, and liquid closure guards are unchanged. The
lower-collapse vector proves continued surface exchange, zero internal
conduction, and aggregate mass/liquid/refrozen conservation. Native-SWE
next-down/equality/next-up tests prove the strict lower comparison side. A
chained retained-state/mass-increase vector proves resume rather than a fresh
state alias. Above-boundary invalid temperature remains fail-closed.

Rejected aliases are distinguishable:

- a temperature clamp would mutate or publish thermal state;
- an epsilon pressure would still evaluate vapor exchange;
- forced melt would reduce layer/SWE mass or increase routed liquid;
- cold-content deletion would change the before/after ledger; and
- one final flux would make at least one energy/vapor accumulator nonzero.

None occurs in the contract-derived tests or exact cohort trace evidence.
