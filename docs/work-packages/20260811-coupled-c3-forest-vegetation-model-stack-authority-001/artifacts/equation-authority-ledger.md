ledgers. `q(a,b,c)=[(b-sqrt(b^2-4ac))/(2a)]` is the smaller real root; a
negative discriminant beyond the scale-aware solver tolerance is a typed domain
error, not zeroed. Areas are ground area unless explicitly marked leaf area.
