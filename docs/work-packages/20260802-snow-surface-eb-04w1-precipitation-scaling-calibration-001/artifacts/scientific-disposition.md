# Scientific And Calibration Disposition

Status: `PASS`

Evidence mode: **Ran + Inference**.

## Decision

`CALIBRATION_LEVER_CONFIRMED / GRID_EXTENSION_RECOMMENDED / NO_PROMOTION`

## What The Experiment Establishes

Uniform total-precipitation scaling is a material calibration lever for all
four open mountain lanes. Increasing the multiplier from `1.0` to the frozen
selection raised median peak-SWE ratios from `0.62` to `1.06` at Mica Creek,
`0.50` to `0.72` at Niwot, `0.47` to `0.80` at Paradise, and `0.39` to `0.68`
at Snowbird. It also reduced the frozen absolute chronology error by 15, 27,
25, and 9 days, respectively.

This is meaningful joint response, not magnitude-only inflation. Mica Creek,
Niwot, and Paradise show especially strong chronology improvement as added
precipitation sustains later storage. Snowbird responds much less in timing.

## Why The Values Are Not Yet Final Calibrations

Mica Creek, Paradise, and Snowbird select `1.5`, the upper frozen boundary, but
that boundary means different things. Paradise and Snowbird remain improving
on both frozen axes, so their joint optima are unresolved. Mica Creek's
magnitude fit turns over after its near-parity `1.4` cell, while its chronology
improves one more day at `1.5`; its unresolved question is the tradeoff, not an
unbracketed magnitude optimum. Niwot selects the nonboundary `1.3` cell under
the frozen lexicographic rule, but its peak-SWE ratio remains only `0.72` and
continues upward through `1.5`; that is evidence of responsiveness, not a
satisfactory magnitude fit.

The storage ledger also prevents an overly simple causal claim. At Snowbird,
the selected cell supplies effective input equal to `1.05` times observed peak
SWE, but retains only `0.55` times observed peak SWE on that date. Additional
precipitation helps, yet pre-peak loss and timing remain materially limiting.
Niwot similarly supplies `1.35` times observed peak input while retaining
`0.47`. Mica Creek nearly closes retained storage at `1.5`; Paradise improves
to `0.73` retained storage and a 12-day chronology error.

## Claim Boundary

These SNOTEL records were prospectively assigned to calibration. The result
does not prove gauge undercatch, spatial representativeness error, or a unique
precipitation deficit; a multiplier may compensate for phase, retention, or
loss errors. It supplies no independent validation, transferability, regional
default, production coefficient, or process-promotion authority.

## Recommended Successor

Run one prospectively frozen grid extension before abandoning the forcing
lever. Paradise and Snowbird still improve on both objective axes at `1.5`,
and Niwot magnitude continues upward through `1.5` even though its frozen rule
selects `1.3` after a chronology tie. Mica Creek is different: magnitude is
closest to parity at `1.4` and worsens slightly at `1.5`, while chronology
improves one more day. Retain `1.0-1.5` anchors, extend upward with a
predeclared overshoot/compensation stop-loss, and preserve the same operators.
The successor should bracket the first three trends and test whether Mica's
small chronology gain persists after magnitude turns over. It must not fit
separate process coefficients or relabel these calibration observations as
validation.
