# Ecosystem-Model Limitation Adjudication

Evidence class: `Ran + Static`

## Adjudication

`ACCEPTED LIMITATION / DEFER FURTHER CANOPY PHENOLOGY WORK`

For the assessed Bezà tropical dry-forest lane, the current temperature × VPD ×
photoperiod GSI formulation does not represent the observed seasonal
transition chronology adequately. The contradiction is robust under both
retained GCC products and their reported uncertainty.

This is adjudicated as an ecosystem-model limitation, not:

- a numerical or executor defect;
- an hourly-VPD reconstruction defect;
- a single observation-product selection error;
- a calibrated parameter defect with an identified correction; or
- authority for surrogate rainfall, soil-water, or hydraulic physics.

## Stop-loss

Do not execute another canopy timing calibration round on the current Bezà
2024–2025 evidence. Do not continue generic literature or institutional data
searches. Retain Order 7 as not passed, close the current investigation chain,
and allow the broader canopy assurance product to report this limitation.

## Scope of the limitation

The adjudication applies to:

- the current CP-GSI01 structure and accepted 37-member ensemble;
- the checksum-bound provisional Bezà ROI and 2024–2025 transitions; and
- claims about tropical dry-forest transition chronology and transferability.

It does not invalidate CP-GSI01 at its calibrated northern deciduous site, nor
does it establish the correct replacement mechanism.

## Reactivation trigger

Reopen only under a new, explicitly authorized ecosystem-phenology package
that brings at least one of:

1. field-corresponded phenology plus colocated water/climate observations;
2. authoritative tropical dry-forest process science sufficient for a
   contract-first model extension; or
3. an independently testable alternative ecosystem phenology formulation with
   a reserved external validation lane.

A newer provisional camera processing run, another threshold sweep, or more
generic analogue literature is not by itself a reactivation trigger.
