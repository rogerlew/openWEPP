## Historical mandatory joins and blockers (all physical blockers superseded)

1. `Q_complete` is an exact-one composition of net shortwave, net longwave,
   sensible, bounded latent, external snow--soil conduction, internal
   active/lower conduction where applicable, and precipitation-advected heat.
   Unavailable components block publication; a producer residual cannot fill a
   missing term.
2. The precipitation mass set and precipitation-advection energy set are the
   same typed parcel set. Covered destinations receive only the terminal V11
   interception releases; open destinations receive only their atmospheric
   raw-rain parcels. Solid ground-snow precipitation remains atmospheric and
   canopy-intercepted snow remains out of scope.
3. `PRECIPITATION PARCEL SCHEMA: CONTRACT AMENDMENT REQUIRED / IN PROGRESS`.
   The amendment must normatively define canonical fields/framing/order,
   destination exclusivity, amount basis conversion, thermodynamic-provider
   identity, producer-state bind, and complete-empty-set semantics.
4. `SNOW--SOIL MAPPING: AUTHORITY_MISSING`. Before code edits, authority must
   select participating soil nodes, lane-level versus destination-keyed
   boundary, whether tile aggregation applies, conductivity/path length, sign, fixed-
   point participation, receipt framing, atomic owner installation and
   rollback. Current authority forbids silently choosing a first tile,
   unweighted or covered-only average, duplicating one lane flux across tiles,
   or suppressing soil heat.
5. The independent outcome ledger is built after the physical candidate and
   may never provide solver operands, convergence acceptance, caps, or a
   replacement residual. It must independently reconstruct beginning,
   transfers, phase changes, endings, topology/basis and receipt cardinality.
