## Candidate same-support formulation

Let `X` contain every active snow lane, all shared algebraic carrier variables,
and the complete seven-owner joint. Let `M` map state increments into their
mass/energy/storage measures, and let `F(t,X)` be one authoritative
owner-complete conservative residual/flux surface.

For exact absolute support `[t0,t1)` with `h=t1-t0 >= 0.6 s`, independently
solve:

```text
low (BE):  M(X_L-X_0) - h F(t1,X_L) = 0
high (CN): M(X_H-X_0) - h/2 [F(t0,X_0) + F(t1,X_H)] = 0
```

Both solves must include identical forcing/topology, phase complementarity,
snow--soil receipts, hydrology transitions, all active lanes, and all seven
owners. Each solve has its own residual-convergence proof. The existing
componentwise mass/energy tolerances remain unchanged and the temporal norm is
the maximum across every lane, shared-owner component, and complete custody
ledger. `X_L` is evidence only; only `X_H` may be installed. No arithmetic
blend or extrapolation may be installed.

This formulation is not yet canonical authority. The live carrier currently
returns integrated transitions and does not expose `F(t0,X0)` and `F(t1,X)`
consistently for every owner. Treating carrier fixed-point iterates, ending
hints, or independently advanced lane states as the embedded pair would be a
surrogate. Production implementation therefore remains prohibited pending
contract admission and two independent `GO` reviews.
