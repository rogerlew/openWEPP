# Observation Operator and Objective

Evidence class: `Static: frozen before result-bearing execution`

Synthetic daily recovery uses the complete emitted post-decay daily
surface-stock series
and unweighted SSE in `kg^2 m^-4`. Terminal-stock distance is used separately
to expose source/decay equifinality. Neither is an empirical objective.

Harvard rows retain their source units and plot identities. The only
transformations are `g C` to `kg C` by division by 1000, pooled-nonfoliar
share `nonfoliar / total`, and annual total-flux/stock ratio
`(total / 1000) / stock`. These are descriptive derived diagnostics with
ratio units `yr^-1`. Every row preserves the 2000-2011 flux period and 2014
stock year. They are not compared with model dry mass, interpreted as
contemporaneous turnover or decay fits, pooled across plots, or weighted with
invented uncertainty.

The full recurrence and chronology are frozen in
`execution-control-contract.md`.
