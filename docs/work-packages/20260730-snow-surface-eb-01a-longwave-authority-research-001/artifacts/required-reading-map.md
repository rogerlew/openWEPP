# Required Reading Map

## Repository authority

- Root `AGENTS.md`: repository-wide scope, science, validation, and truthfulness
  rules.
- `docs/work-packages/AGENTS.md`: package execution, review, verification, and
  closure rules.
- `SC-SNOWFREEZE-001`: current snow forcing, energy, and candidate boundaries.
- `SC-CLIMATE-001`: daily-to-hourly radiation, air temperature, dewpoint, and
  cloud-fraction lineage.
- `SNOW-SURFACE-EB-01`: the four-cell design and authority-gap ledger.

## Primary science

- Essery et al. (2008), DOI `10.1002/hyp.6930`: hemispherical view-factor
  integral, two-component equation, and canopy-air temperature error
  approximation.
- Sicart et al. (2004), DOI
  `10.1175/1525-7541(2004)005<0774:ASSODN>2.0.CO;2`: forest-snow net-radiation
  sensitivity and near-unity vegetation/snow emissivity.
- Flerchinger et al. (2009), DOI `10.1029/2008WR007394`: multisite comparison
  of atmospheric longwave algorithms and uncertainty.
- Lundquist et al. (2013), DOI `10.1002/wrcr.20504`: operational forest-snow
  composition and limitations of the air-temperature proxy.
- Webster et al. (2016), DOI `10.1002/2015JD024099`: two- versus three-part
  canopy/trunk treatment and high-insolation errors.
- Musselman and Pomeroy (2017), DOI `10.1175/JHM-D-16-0111.1`: physically
  resolved needle/trunk temperatures, view factors, and spatial influence.
- Rutter et al. (2023), DOI `10.1029/2022JD037980`: modern continuous-stand
  evaluation, leaf-off birch evidence, sky-view dominance, and stable-night
  temperature decoupling.
- Essery et al. (2025), DOI `10.5194/gmd-18-3583-2025`: FSM2 effective
  vegetation area, diffuse transmission, and reuse of that transmission for
  longwave radiation.
