# Kickoff

Scaffold and execute a pre-EB-02 science research package that resolves
authoritative sub-canopy longwave/view-factor and canopy-temperature
formulations. Ask the user to acquire articles only if unavailable source bytes
remain load-bearing.

This kickoff explicitly authorizes subagent spawning/delegation for two
independent read-only review subagents and two independent read-only
terminal-verification subagents as required by the package. Their only
writable paths are, respectively,
`artifacts/review-agent-a.md`, `artifacts/review-agent-b.md`,
`artifacts/verification-agent-a.md`, and
`artifacts/verification-agent-b.md`. They may not edit production code,
canonical contracts, fixtures, or roadmap decisions.
