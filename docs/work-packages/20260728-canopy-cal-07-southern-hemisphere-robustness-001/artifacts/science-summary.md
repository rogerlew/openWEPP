# CAL-07 Science Summary

Evidence class: `Ran + Static`

## What was established

Two Southern Hemisphere site assignments independent of CAL-04B fitting were
admitted before execution:

- a deciduous broadleaf tropical dry-forest ROI at Beza Mahafaly,
  Madagascar; and
- an evergreen temperate-forest ROI at Alerce Costero, Chile.

The admitted camera records contain 934 and 925 quality-filtered raw GCC90
days, respectively, across multiple seasons. They support future relative
timing and shape evaluation, with the existing limitations that both products
share one camera-processing method, are provisional, and do not measure
absolute foliage amount.

## What execution discovered

The gridded NASA POWER operands are not fully compatible with the production
plant contract. OBL-PLANT-P-013 reconstructs negative VPD on three Alerce
days. The executor rejected the first such day before publishing any daily
canopy result. Independent reconstruction confirmed all three failures.

This is an input-domain/authority result, not evidence that Southern
Hemisphere phenology is wrong. It also cannot be treated as harmless warm-up
noise because one failure occurs during 2025, a planned scoring year.

## Scientific disposition

CAL-07 is `HOLD / FORCING AUTHORITY INCOMPATIBLE`. Timing, relative shape,
mass closure, and consumer results are `NOT_EVALUATED`. Absolute canopy
amplitude, quantitative evergreen floor, and phase-transformed downstream
consumer chronology were already outside the bounded protocol's evidence
ceiling and remain open.

The scientifically valid next input is continuous site forcing that satisfies
the existing contract. The alternative is an explicit contract amendment
authorizing bounded canonicalization; it must define the threshold, units,
physical rationale, provenance, and tests before any new result execution.
The current package supplies no authority for clipping negative VPD.
