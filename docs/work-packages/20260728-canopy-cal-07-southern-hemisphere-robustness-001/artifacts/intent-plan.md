# CAL-07 Frozen Intent

Frozen before result execution on 2026-07-28.

CAL-07 evaluates, but does not tune, the 37-member CAL-04B ensemble. The two
independent lanes are Beza Mahafaly deciduous broadleaf dry forest in
Madagascar and Alerce Costero evergreen forest in Chile. The former supplies
relative greenness and transition-timing proxies; the latter supplies an
independent evergreen-class persistence boundary. NASA POWER supplies the
daily temperature/dew-point forcing used to reconstruct contract-defined VPD.

No result may change the sites, ensemble, observation filters, transition
pairing, score definitions, or advancement rules in `package.md`. Complete
calendar-year shape scores are restricted to 2024 and 2025; partial seasons
remain visual context only. The package explicitly withholds Order 7 because
absolute amplitude, quantitative evergreen floor, and phase-transformed real
consumer chronology are not admitted. A necessary change requires an incident
record and renewed prospective review before result execution.
