# Caption

**CAL-06 canopy chronology.** Daily canopy cover, leaf-area index, and
21-day growing-season index for the Marcell, Harvard, and Hubbard Brook
strata. Lines show ensemble medians and shaded bands span the complete frozen
37-member CAL-04B timing ensemble.

Paired figure: `cal06-canopy-chronology.svg`.

## Ancillary information

- The open controls execute once and therefore do not have an ensemble spread.
- Cover and GSI21 are fractions; LAI is expressed in m²/m².
- Color and line pattern jointly identify open, deciduous, mixed, and conifer
  strata.
- These curves characterize model response. They do not independently
  validate canopy-amplitude operands or repair CAL-04B's poor Harvard timing
  transferability.
