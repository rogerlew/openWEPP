# Caption

**CAL-06 seasonal ordering and amplitude.** Ensemble median winter cover,
summer cover, seasonal cover amplitude, and summer LAI by site and stratum.
Whiskers span all accepted timing members.

Paired figure: `cal06-seasonal-ordering-amplitude.svg`.

## Ancillary information

- Winter cover ordering is preserved by 37/37 members for Marcell deciduous
  < mixed < conifer, Harvard deciduous < mixed, and Hubbard Brook deciduous
  < mixed.
- Open bars are single-run controls rather than timing-ensemble members.
- Cover quantities are fractions; LAI is expressed in m²/m².
- Stable ordering is bounded within-model evidence, not empirical validation
  of foliar mass, evergreen fraction, LAI, structural cover, or closure.
