# Authority Boundary Decision

## Decision

Site-specific stratum parameter values, topology, and complete compatible
initial state are caller-supplied configuration. A0 authority governs schema
semantics, units, scale, domains, required presence, equations, guards,
ownership, and conservation. Demonstration values may be
`ASSUMED_FOR_EXECUTION` and prove behavior only.

The native-forest route does not preserve the agricultural WEPP PMET
`Kcb`/LAI partition. It requires separately owned canopy transpiration,
wet-canopy evaporation, forest-floor evaporation, and layer-root uptake, and
forbids automatic donation of lost canopy demand to soil evaporation.

## Claim Boundary

This decision admits neither a constitutive equation family nor any empirical
site value. Distributed defaults, site recommendations, calibration,
validation, and transferability claims still require independent evidence.
