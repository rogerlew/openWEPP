# Pressure and Humidity Selection

FAO-56 station-elevation pressure is the static climate-lineage owner. Actual
vapor pressure is Harder--Pomeroy liquid saturation at dew point. Specific
humidity is the exact CLM inverse; VPD is saturation at hourly air temperature
minus actual vapor pressure and is never clipped. Nonpositive VPD is typed
unsupported for vegetation while physical humidity remains available to LSE.
