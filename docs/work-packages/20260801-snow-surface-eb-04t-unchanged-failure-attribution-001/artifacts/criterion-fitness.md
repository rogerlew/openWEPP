# Promotion-Criterion Fitness

Evidence mode: `Static + Reused Ran`.

Disposition: `CONSERVATIVE_BUT_MIXED_ALIGNMENT`.

The total robust-failure reduction criterion mixed two questions:

1. Do canopy longwave and sublimation improve mechanism-identifying snow-energy
   and mass-loss signatures?
2. Does the combined model also repair inherited density/geometry failures?

Eleven of 16 failures primarily answer the second question, so requiring their
repair is not a clean efficacy test. They remain valuable collateral-state
guards because LS perturbs them and moves their primary errors away rather than
toward observations.

The remaining five failures concern peak and melt-out timing, but all are open
SNOTEL controls. Four move away from observation and one is unchanged under LS.
They provide legitimate evidence about sublimation and combined-bundle
noninferiority, but cannot identify canopy-longwave efficacy. Removing the 11
density/geometry rows would therefore still leave no affirmative basis to
promote the combined LS default, while saying nothing decisive about longwave
alone.

The rule was conservative but not a mere governance technicality. Its exact
direction counts have no frozen materiality threshold and must not be described
as practical degradation. It should not be rewritten retrospectively. A future
prospective rule should separately test canopy-longwave efficacy, sublimation
efficacy, and adjacent-process noninferiority on independent,
mechanism-identifying evidence.
