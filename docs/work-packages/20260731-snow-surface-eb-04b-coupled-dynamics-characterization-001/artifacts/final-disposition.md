# Final Disposition

Status: `COMPLETE / PASS`

Dual independent terminal verification: `PASS / PASS`

EB-04B closes as a characterization-only package without changing production
physics or rerunning the frozen EB-04 experiment. It retains an exact
83,232-row chronology and assigns all 24 EB-04A failures:

- 17 impossible thermal states caused by positive cold content on a vanishing
  control-volume mass;
- five valid-Kelvin states that underflow in the SNOBAL saturation-vapor-
  pressure dependency; and
- two geometry states caused by a dimensionally inconsistent fragment filter
  and depth-closure boundary.

Sublimation is strongly associated and mechanistically contributory in 20/22
thermal cases but is not necessary, and the unpublished crossing substep makes
terminal amplification inconclusive. EB-04C and EB-04D are independently
admitted; EB-04C is recommended first. No correction, calibration, guard
relaxation, or promotion is authorized by this disposition.
