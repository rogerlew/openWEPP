# Compaction contributions by lane

## Caption

Four-cell pooled mean of exact daily density-ledger contributions. The figure shows whether wet compaction, destructive metamorphism, or overburden dominates each retained density failure.

## Population and method

Nine immutable EB-04U density-failure lanes, each executed under B, L, S, and LS. Values are in kg m^-3 per applicable modeled snow day and derive from the real `openwepp-r7h-direct-production-snow-trace-v2` consumer.

## Interpretation limits

Bars are diagnostic contributions from the executed B/L/S/LS traces, not fitted effects or unique causal estimates. EB-04V changes no physics and makes no promotion claim.
