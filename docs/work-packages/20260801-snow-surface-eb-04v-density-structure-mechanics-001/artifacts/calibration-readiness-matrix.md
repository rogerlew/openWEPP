## Mechanics classification

Wet compaction dominates the three positive compaction terms. Destructive
metamorphism is small in the pooled daily means, while overburden is material
but much smaller than wet compaction. Fresh-snow mixing, structural projection,
and caps provide large opposing density changes; Stage 3 is comparatively
small in the pooled mean. The under-density group combines stronger positive
wet/overburden compaction with stronger negative fresh-mixing/runtime-cap
effects. This rules out inactive compaction, but it does not rule out
insufficient compaction relative to forcing and opposing terms. A one-direction
coefficient adjustment is not identified.

## Successor admission

EB-04V closes the observability prerequisite but holds result-bearing density
calibration. A future candidate needs unused independent observations or a
prospectively acquired cohort, numeric materiality/noninferiority thresholds,
replication and site-spread rules, and a pre-result candidate identity tied to
one authority-backed mechanism. EB-04W may proceed as the next bounded
diagnostic campaign item; it is not admitted as a density efficacy round.
