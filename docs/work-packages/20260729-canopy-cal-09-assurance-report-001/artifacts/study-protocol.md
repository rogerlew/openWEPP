# Study Protocol

Status: `FROZEN`

## Question

Does openWEPP's native-forest canopy formulation produce internally correct
seasonal states, and where is that behavior corroborated, bounded, or
contradicted by available evidence?

## Coherent-Study Test

One integrated report is retained because daily phenology state is the common
cause linking foliage, litter, residue, and downstream consumers. Evidence
roles and claim envelopes remain separate in sections and the claim matrix;
no pooled grade is calculated.

## Domains And Operators

Temperate evidence covers Hubbard Brook calibration and Harvard and Marcell
comparisons. Canopy-gradient classes are deciduous, mixed, conifer where
source-supplied, plus open controls. Southern evidence includes Alerce forcing
diagnostics and the Bezà tropical dry-forest 2024-2025 provisional camera ROI.

Timing evaluation preserves daily chronology and same-direction transition
crossings. Camera GCC is a relative greenness product, not LAI or foliar mass.
CAL-05 compares complete daily stock and one finite-horizon terminal stock.
CAL-06 retains exact-date snow metrics where units are admissible; Harvard SWE
is excluded for its unit/identity contradiction.

## Uncertainty And Stop Rules

Retain accepted-ensemble covariance and observation intervals. Do not interpret
search bounds as physiological bounds, snow residuals as canopy residuals, or
synthetic recovery as empirical calibration. Stop calibration when the
prospective CAL-07F criteria fail. Reopen Bezà science only with
field-corresponded evidence, authoritative process science, or an independently
testable alternative formulation.

## Exclusions

No new physics, refit, literature campaign, predictive needle/fine-woody law,
downstream compensation, application approval, human identity, public
publication, or release transfer is authorized.
