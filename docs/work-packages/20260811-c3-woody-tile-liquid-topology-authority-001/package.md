## Scientific Selection

For each occupied pair `(s,t)`, V2 owns one `S_liq,s,t` in
`kg H2O m^-2 tile-ground`. Conditional plant area is
`LAI_s,t=LAI_s/C_s`, `WAI_s,t=WAI_s/C_s`, where
`C_s=sum_{t in T_s} f_t`. E04 and wet-surface/coupled physiology execute locally.
Free throughfall and both drainage terms proceed to the next lower occupancy in
the same tile; stemflow bypasses lower foliage to that tile's ground recipient.
Only completed tile-local fluxes are weighted by `f_t` for stand-ground owners.

Water resource identity is `(transaction, owner, occupancy, layer, resource,
amount_basis)`. Tile demand is weighted once before arbitration; authorization
is divided by `f_t` once for the constrained local re-solve; finalized local use
is weighted once on return. Shared stratum C/N advances once from weighted
accepted occupancy fluxes.
