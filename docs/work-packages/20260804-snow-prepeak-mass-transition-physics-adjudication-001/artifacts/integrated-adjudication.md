## Why This Verdict Follows

1. The exact storage, shared handoff, and Stage-3 identities close at maximum
   residuals of `9.9973e-13 m`, `1.3878e-17 m`, and `2.5045e-17 m`. There is no
   second authoritative SWE debit.
2. Gross-positive applied CoE melt tracks authoritative pack loss at all four
   sites: median ratios are `1.0065`, `1.0176`, `0.9995`, and `1.0132`. This is
   adjacent-ledger localization, not independent proof of excessive melt.
3. Static real-consumer evidence proves Stage-3 routed/retained/refrozen
   outcomes are bypassed by authoritative runoff publication and do not restore
   runtime SWE. A downstream-retention correction cannot fix the current peak
   deficit without first changing state and consumer authority.
4. The daily-local signed opportunity is positive in all `154/154` eligible
   primary site-years, but its frozen `>= 0.25` site-median materiality screen
   passes only Mica Creek and Niwot. Paradise and Snowbird are only `0.130` and
   `0.140`. Signed-hour asymmetry is real and worth preserving in the next
   design, but it is not a cohort-wide sufficient explanation.
5. The post-CoE Stage-3 cold-content index is small: site medians are only
   `0.00455-0.0137` of the positive peak deficit. It is also a temporally reused,
   feedback-free index measured after the CoE debit. It cannot support a
   cold-content recovery claim.
6. Producer Stage-3 capture throughput is only `0.0227-0.0343` of incoming
   liquid. Endpoint layer-liquid change is smaller still. These values describe
   a diagnostic shadow; their low magnitude is not the causal reason they are
   excluded.

- how one total snow-water store should own ice, retained liquid, refrozen
  liquid, capacity contraction, and downstream release;

  geometry; and
- any transferable parameter or production correction.

The recommended successor is a contract-first upstream CoE/total-snow-water
authority package. It should establish one physically authoritative state and
a real consumer before testing an equation change. Signed-hour handling,
pre-debit cold content, duplicated capacity ownership, and wet-compaction
forcing belong in that coupled design; none is authorized for implementation
here.
