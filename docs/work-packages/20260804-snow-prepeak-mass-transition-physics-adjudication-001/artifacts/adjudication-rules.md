# Adjudication Rules

Status: `scaffold rules superseded before result execution`

Evidence mode: `Static`

The scaffold rules below are retained as custody evidence but are superseded by
the exact operators and truth table in `analysis-freeze-v2.json`. The v2 rules
retain the numerical thresholds while preventing descriptive Stage-3
throughput and adjacent-ledger tracking from being mistaken for independent
causal evidence.

- `UPSTREAM_GENERATION_PRIORITY`: gross-positive applied CoE melt tracks
  solid-pack loss under the frozen screen, while downstream stored/refrozen
  fraction fails the dominance screen.
- `PREEXPORT_ENTHALPY_OR_SIGNED_HOUR_PRIORITY`: at least one frozen
  opportunity screen is systemic/material and exact ordering shows liquid is
  offered downstream before that state can constrain the upstream transition.
- `DOWNSTREAM_RETENTION_REFREEZE_PRIORITY`: not selectable when exact static
  consumer evidence confirms the Stage-3 result is snow-neutral and bypassed
  by hydrologic publication.
- `MULTIFACTOR_PRIORITY`: more than one family passes independently and no
  single family explains the site/year population.
- `UNRESOLVED`: the required signatures conflict, fail coverage, or lack
  physical authority.

Only the daily-local signed opportunity may trigger the v2 quantitative
pre-export rule. Window-wide signed cancellation, post-CoE Stage-3 cold
content, and positive Stage-3 capture are secondary descriptive indices.
Forcing/phase error remains a named uncertainty because this package does not
replay external forcing. No verdict may claim that forcing is correct. A
diagnostic opportunity index must never be described as modeled peak-SWE
recovery.
