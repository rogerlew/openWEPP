# CAL-04 Prior Disposition

Status: `EXECUTED / PENDING REVIEW`

CAL-04 adopts a prior-free deterministic design. No probability distribution
is asserted for any canopy operand.

The fitting package must enumerate a reproducible grid or bounded ensemble,
then retain objective profiles and equifinal ranges. Hard execution domains
may come from typed schemas and controlling science contracts. Evidence-based
plausibility bounds may be added only through a new prospective admission.
Current native values and Bill Elliot operands are initialization or comparison
points, not priors.

Grid density, refinement rules, stopping rules, objective components, and
boundary-hit handling must be frozen before fitting. A profile that remains
flat or terminates at a domain boundary is reported as `NONIDENTIFIABLE` or
`BOUNDARY_HIT`; it is not repaired by inventing a prior.

This prospective choice lifts the probability-prior blocker without claiming
that the retained evidence supplies scientific probability.
