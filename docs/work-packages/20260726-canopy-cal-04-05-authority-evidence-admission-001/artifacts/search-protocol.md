# Search Protocol

Status: `EXECUTED`

Local discovery covered the retained canopy literature, CAL-01 through CAL-03
ledgers, fixture observations, and mounted `/workdir`/`/home/workdir`
resources. External discovery was limited to primary government, LTER/EDI,
Harvard Forest, USDA Forest Service, NEON, PhenoCam/ORNL, and identified
publisher records.

CAL-04 inclusion required quantitative transition observations, complete
methods and terms, northern deciduous/mixed applicability, and a calibration
versus independent-site holdout split. CAL-05 inclusion required measured
annual material flux and comparable forest-floor stock with explicit material
and measurement bases.

Duplicates and syntheses were retained only when they exposed source objects
or matching information. Search stopped for a gap after two independent query
families and the primary site catalog converged on the same objects, or after
an admissible object satisfied the gap. Access-controlled redundant objects
were not pursued after stronger open objects were admitted.

Metadata alone supported discovery, not admission. Admission required original
data, EML methods, terms, checksum retention, and independent review.
