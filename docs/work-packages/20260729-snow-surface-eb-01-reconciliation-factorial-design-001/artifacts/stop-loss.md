# Campaign Stop-Loss

Status: `binding campaign boundary`; EB-04 outcome thresholds are an explicit
pre-execution hold.

The machine-readable triggers and exact outcomes are frozen in
[stop-loss.csv](stop-loss.csv). The operator, gate, threshold/window owner, and
failure outcome for future result interpretation are in
[decision-rules.csv](decision-rules.csv).

Stop mechanism development and adjudicate the unresolved behavior as a model
limitation when any of these conditions holds:

1. EB-02 cannot admit a load-bearing canopy/sky longwave formulation after the
   two prioritized authority-acquisition routes (priorities 1 and 2) are
   exhausted. Priority 3 is transfer data, not process authority.
2. EB-03 cannot express latent heat and vapor mass as one independently
   reconstructable transfer without changing unrelated snow physics.
3. Any candidate fails mass closure, energy closure, exact-one latent/mass
   equivalence, or trace identity.
4. Improvement requires site-specific forcing correction, unregistered
   calibration, an unbounded parameter, a different common model between
   factorial cells, or revival of a rejected candidate without new authority.
5. After one pre-registered EB-04 factorial round, no admissible cell improves
   the prospectively frozen primary forcing-robust rubric without increasing
   the forcing-robust failure count, failing a physical gate, or regressing a
   protected control/stratum under the frozen tie policy.
6. A second calibration round would be proposed without a new identifying
   observation, a new authoritative equation, or a falsifiable residual
   attribution.

EB-04 is not executable while any hard-gate tolerance, snow-disappearance
threshold/persistence window, runoff window, or tie rule in
`decision-rules.csv` remains assigned to a successor instead of a frozen
value. EB-01 does not invent those values. This is a named prerequisite, not a
claim that undefined “material improvement” is already operational.

The campaign may report narrower supported findings even when a transfer claim
stops. In particular, missing warm-maritime conifer data blocks that
transferability claim, not authoritative contract work or cold-continental and
hardwood/open evaluation.
