# CAL-07E Authority Disposition

Evidence class: `Static synthesis`

## Decision

`LITERATURE REVIEW COMPLETE / METHOD AUDIT AUTHORIZED / SCIENCE CORRECTION NOT AUTHORIZED`

The admitted literature narrows the CAL-07D contradiction but does not resolve
it with a unique causal mechanism or parameterization.

## Admitted conclusions

- Rainfall is a supported contributor to seasonal leaf abundance at Bezà.
- Gallery and xerophytic forest have materially different seasonal leaf
  retention.
- Water stress, stored plant water, photoperiod, and hydraulic strategy are
  credible contributors with different levels of regional or analogue support.
- The retained transition dates and ancillary GCC series use different
  PhenoCam products.
- GCC relative-amplitude transitions cannot be equated directly with GSI or a
  structural canopy fraction.

## Not authorized

- a VPD, rainfall, soil-water, groundwater, photoperiod, or stored-water
  production equation;
- a corrected GSI threshold or tropical dry-forest parameter set;
- a NASA POWER forcing bias correction;
- selecting `gcc_mean` or `gcc_90` according to model agreement;
- treating 2024–2025 diagnostic dates as independent validation after fitting;
  or
- lifting canopy assurance Order 7.

## Recommended sequence

1. Execute a bounded PhenoCam transition-product and observation-operator audit
   using checksum-frozen standard Data Record 5, uncertainty intervals,
   provisional-status labeling, and separate `gcc_mean`/`gcc_90` lanes.
2. Seek the 2014 thesis and post-2011 direct-site field/weather records.
3. If acquired, admit and quality-control those records before deciding between
   forcing, ecotype, or missing-process work.
4. Only then prepare a contract-first science package if a process addition is
   actually supported.

## Roadmap effect

CAL-07E closes the literature question while retaining the CAL-07 Order 7 hold.
The next bounded package should be CAL-07F, an observation-product/operator
audit. It is not a calibration package and must not alter production behavior.
