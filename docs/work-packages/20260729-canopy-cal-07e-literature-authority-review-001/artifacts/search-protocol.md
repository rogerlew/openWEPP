# CAL-07E Search Protocol

Evidence class: `Static`

## Search lanes

1. Exact site: `"Bezà Mahafaly" phenology`, leafing, leaf fall, rainfall,
   temperature, groundwater, water stress, and vegetation monitoring.
2. Regional: Madagascar dry forest and spiny thicket, prioritizing
   southwestern/southern Tsimanampetsotsa and Andohahela evidence and
   separately limiting more distant western-Madagascar baobab evidence.
3. Method: PhenoCam GCC90, spline transitions, 10/25/50 percent amplitude,
   uncertainty, and in-situ comparison.
4. Mechanism: primary tropical dry-forest tests of rainfall, soil/plant water,
   stored water, photoperiod, temperature, and atmospheric demand.

Search discovery may use scholarly indexes and institutional catalogs, but
claim-bearing evidence must resolve to a primary publication or authoritative
dataset/method record with accessible full text.

## Inclusion

- Directly measures or analyzes leaf phenology, relevant environmental forcing,
  or PhenoCam transition behavior.
- Reports study location, observation design, and result basis.
- Has sufficient full text to distinguish result from author interpretation.
- Materially addresses a CAL-07D solution route.

## Exclusion

- Search snippets, encyclopedias, or unattributed web summaries.
- Studies limited to flowering/fruiting unless they also constrain leafing or
  the observation method.
- Remote regions or vegetation types without a clearly labeled analogue role.
- Modeling studies that do not add independent observed mechanism evidence.

## Stop rule

Stop a lane after:

- the exact-site publication catalog and backward citations are exhausted;
- at least two independent primary method sources cover transition extraction
  and field/remote agreement;
- at least two regional primary sources and two mechanism analogues have
  been appraised; and
- two successive targeted searches add no new claim category.

An inaccessible direct-site thesis, field dataset, or article remains
`ACQUISITION_NEEDED`; it does not prevent synthesis from accessible sources but
limits the authority ceiling.
