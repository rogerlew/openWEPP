### Candidate decision

`NO_IMPLEMENTATION_WARRANTED`.

The largest measured primary operation is required capability sealing/provider
binding. Removing, caching or weakening it would change validation/custody
semantics and is outside this envelope. The largest behavior-preserving
materialization candidate, the 0.213 s snow-free envelope, has a cost-free
whole-run ceiling of 4.28%, below the prioritization target, and the retained
fixed-point, publication and custody work means its credible saving is lower.

The concrete repeated-computation candidate is to compute hourly forcing and
daily extraterrestrial radiation once per day instead of 48 times. Here
`f = 0.00000514` of runner time, retained work is 1/48 of those projections,
and a plausible local acceleration is `s = 48`; modeled whole-run saving is
`f * (1 - 1/s) = 0.00000503`, or about 0.000503%. Its theoretical cost-free
ceiling is about 0.000514%. That evidence does not warrant even the authorized
small experimental patch. Destination materialization and interval cloning are
also only 0.00818% combined before retained validation.

The alternative remains 14.47%, but source inspection shows no bounded
behavior-preserving removable operation: it constructs the physical envelope
and required evidence consumed by downstream precipitation, boundary, soil and
custody checks. The workload did not activate the full provisional physical
diagnostic audit, so removing that observer would save 0 and would not be a
production modeling improvement. The always-active compact mechanism audits
remain experiment-only overhead shared by both timing postures; their total
cost was not isolated and is not subtracted or called a speedup.
