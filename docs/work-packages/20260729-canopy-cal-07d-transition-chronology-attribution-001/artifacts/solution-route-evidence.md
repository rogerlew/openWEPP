# CAL-07D Solution Routes And Required Evidence

Evidence class: `Static synthesis of Ran diagnostics`

## Observation-operator route

The relative-level audit shows that absolute `GSI21=0.5` exaggerates missing
crossings. A future observation-operator package needs the PhenoCam fit method,
raw image/ROI review, fit metadata, transition uncertainty or field phenology
corroboration, and a prospectively frozen mapping. It must reserve validation
data and cannot infer that GCC measures GSI, LAI, biomass, or canopy cover.

## Parameter/ecotype route

Photoperiod and VPD sensitivity make a tropical dry-forest parameter stratum
plausible, but CAL-07D identifies no threshold. A calibration package needs
site/ecotype threshold authority, complete forcing, and an independently
reserved tropical dry-forest site or held-out years. The current Beza
diagnostic rows cannot simultaneously calibrate and validate that stratum.

## Forcing route

NASA POWER is gridded/reanalysis evidence rather than on-site meteorology.
Discriminating forcing bias requires quality-controlled on-site daily minimum
temperature, humidity or VPD, and precipitation across complete leaf-off and
leaf-on seasons. A forcing correction must be source-admitted before it is
used to judge physiology.

## Missing-process route

Observed rising transitions occur while photoperiod and VPD jointly suppress
BASE GSI. That is compatible with a rainfall, soil-water, plant-hydraulic, or
other tropical dry-forest cue, but it is not evidence for an equation. This
route needs:

- site/ecotype literature establishing the environmental cue;
- precipitation plus soil-water or plant-water-status observations at
  defensible rooting/canopy scales;
- field or image phenology corroboration; and
- a second Southern Hemisphere deciduous lane or held-out years.

Only admitted science authority could justify a later contract-first process
package. CAL-07D does not authorize surrogate water-availability physics.

## Useful article acquisition

The most useful articles or reports would address Beza Mahafaly or comparable
southwestern Madagascar dry-forest leaf flushing/senescence, cue attribution
(photoperiod, rainfall, stored water, soil moisture, or hydraulic state), and
field validation of camera greenness transitions. PhenoCam transition-fitting
method authority and any site-specific phenology study are higher priority
than generic GSI literature.
