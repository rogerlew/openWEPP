# CAL-07D Science Summary

Evidence class: `Ran + Static`

## Result

CAL-07D independently reconstructed all 61,642 CAL-07C Beza member-days with
maximum equation residual `0.000e+00` and reproduced all 148 absolute
transition rows, including the same 11 matches. The CAL-07C chronology
contradiction is therefore not an analyzer or GSI-reconstruction artifact.

## Observation-scale attribution

Replacing absolute `GSI21=0.5` with assumption-bounded event-year relative
model levels recovered 262 member/event/source-level rows that were unmatched
under the absolute comparison. This establishes observation scale as a
mathematical contributor to missing crossings, but it does not resolve
chronology:

- source/model level `0.50` still found no rising match in either year;
- lower rising levels found more matches but were generally late;
- falling relative-level matches remained early; and
- match counts and residuals changed sharply across the 11-level sensitivity
  screen.

No model level is selected or calibrated. PhenoCam transition level and model
state remain only a retrospective analogy.

## Indicator attribution

The observed date-50 rising transitions occur while the BASE ensemble is
strongly constrained:

| Event | Median `iTmin` | Median `iVPD` | Median `iPhoto` | Median instantaneous GSI | Median `GSI21` |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2024 rising | 0.933 | 0.514 | 0.041 | 0.020 | 0.003 |
| 2025 rising | 1.000 | 0.274 | 0.107 | 0.027 | 0.006 |

Photoperiod was the unique minimum indicator for 36 of 37 members at the 2024
date-50 rise and 34 of 37 at the 2025 rise; VPD was minimum for the remaining
members. At falling dates, photoperiod was already zero or near zero for most
or all members, so modeled GSI had largely reached dormancy while source
greenness was still declining.

Temperature removal left aggregate match counts and residuals essentially
unchanged. Photoperiod and VPD removals materially changed event states,
crossing availability, and crossing dates.

## Counterfactual interpretation

- VPD removal produced all 148 event-year relative-midpoint matches, but
  falling medians remained 45--66 days early and rising medians 40--51 days
  late.
- Photoperiod removal produced 106 of 148 absolute matches. Falling medians
  improved to about 15--18 days early; available rising medians remained about
  23--43 days early.
- Removing both photoperiod and VPD eliminated absolute 0.5 crossings because
  trajectories often stayed above the threshold. This demonstrates nonlinear
  crossing sensitivity.
- The single canonical generalized-default trajectory crossed all four
  events, but absolute falling residuals were about +21 and +43 days while
  rising residuals were about -39 and -48 days.

These counterfactuals establish mathematical sensitivity, not correct
parameters or process physics.

## Scientific disposition

The most defensible conclusion is:

- observation scale is a supported contributor;
- photoperiod and VPD are supported mathematical contributors;
- current GSI constraint/threshold sensitivity is plausible but unresolved;
- POWER forcing bias remains plausible and unresolved;
- a missing tropical dry-forest cue remains plausible and unresolved; and
- forcing bias, ecotype physiology, parameter transfer, and missing process
  cannot be separated with current evidence.

Order 7 remains held. CAL-07D does not authorize a parameter refit, production
forcing change, rainfall/soil-water extension, or science-contract amendment.
