## Caption

Median water-year SWE trajectories for the four EB-04W2 selected precipitation
cells. The black line is the retained direct-production result; the two gray
lines are a withdrawn within-snowbench legacy/albedo comparison.

## Methods And Units

SWE is meters of water equivalent converted to millimeters. Curves are medians by day since 1 October across all modeled water years.

The admissible black populations are Mica 23 water years (`2003-2025`), Niwot
20 (`2006-2025`), Paradise 19 (`2007-2025`), and Snowbird 22
(`2003-2025`, with 2008 absent). Each day-of-water-year ordinate is a median;
the plot does not show interannual spread. Gray harness curves are visually
de-emphasized raw failure evidence, not estimates.

## Interpretation Limits

The black direct-production anchor remains descriptive calibration evidence.
The snowbench lines are
not admissible counterfactuals or validation because their SWE ledgers fail by
`0.0377-0.0708 m`; see `../harness-closure-investigation.md`.
