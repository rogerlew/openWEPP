# Residual Chronology Contrast

> **Withdrawn comparison:** the gray harness chronology bars are

> retained direct-production bars remain admissible.

## Caption

Absolute median chronology error for the retained production anchor and both diagnostic snowbench melt paths.

## Methods And Units

Mica Creek and Paradise use melt-out date. Niwot uses the worse of peak-depth and peak-SWE dates. Snowbird uses peak-SWE date. Units are days.

Direct populations are Mica 23 water years (`2003-2025`), Niwot 20
(`2006-2025`; two operators yield 40 windows), Paradise 19 (`2007-2025`), and
Snowbird 22 (`2003-2025`, with 2008 absent). Bars are absolute medians and do
not show interannual direction or spread. Gray harness bars are de-emphasized
raw failure evidence.

## Interpretation Limits

The two gray harness series used identical inputs but do not conserve those
inputs. Common-mode loss changes subsequent state, so neither the values nor
their difference supports an albedo conclusion. See
`../harness-closure-investigation.md`.
