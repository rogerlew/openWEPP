## Purpose / Big Picture

Replace the undifferentiated 16-failure promotion gate with a prospective,
mechanistically partitioned study design. A future investigator should be able
to determine whether a failure begins in snow accumulation, density/layer
evolution, canopy interception, sublimation, or ablation energetics before
selecting an authority-backed process amendment or a new promotion experiment.

EB-04U does not repair physics or rerun a candidate. It freezes the questions,
evidence roles, diagnostic operators, materiality rules, and successor gates
before new result-bearing work begins.

## Objective

Reconstruct the exact EB-04T failure population and partition it into three
mechanistic cohorts: nine diffuse density-trajectory failures, two paired
Harvard depth–SWE geometry/interception failures, and five open-control mountain
under-persistence failures. For each cohort, define seasonal phases,
observation operators, required mass/energy/state operands, available-evidence
status, causal decision paths, calibration/validation roles, and the evidence
that may admit EB-04V, EB-04W, or EB-04X implementation or research work.

## Implementation Intent

Intent: `calibration-readiness + independent-validation design`.

Science implementation: `NOT_APPLICABLE` for EB-04U because no production
process is implemented or amended. Execution must assess readiness and
identifiability separately for every proposed successor mechanism.

No empirical calibration, parameter fitting, candidate execution, production
default change, or promotion decision is authorized.

## Context And Orientation

EB-04S retained the corrected B/L/S/LS factorial and closed nonpromotion under
its frozen total-failure rule. EB-04T then showed why that result cannot be read
as a single process verdict. Eleven failures measure density/geometry or mixed
interception debt. Five timing failures are open controls that can test
sublimation and the combined bundle but cannot identify canopy longwave.

The earlier post-partition residual decomposition found a diffuse density-shape
cluster, a mountain under-persistence cluster, and a paired Harvard
depth-per-SWE cluster. EB-04U turns those retrospective findings into a
prospective study contract. “Prospective” means all operators, evidence roles,
materiality boundaries, candidate-admission rules, and stop conditions are
frozen before a successor inspects new candidate results.

## Phase Plan

### Phase A — Freeze population, terms, and evidence roles

Bind exact retained inputs, reconstruct the 16 rows, preserve the `9/2/5`
cohort partition, define seasonal phases, and assign all measured observations
to nonoverlapping evidence roles. Record rejected aliases and leakage controls.

### Phase B — Build mechanism-discriminating operators

For density, separate KGE correlation, bias, and variability and define phase-
conditioned load/temperature/liquid-water/snow-age reads. For geometry, prove
the SWE–density–depth identity before interpreting forest/open differences. For
under-persistence, distinguish mass missing before peak from snow removed too
early after accumulation using independently sourced operands.

### Phase C — Audit observability and ownership

Map every required operand to existing retained output, reproducible derived
data, or a named observability gap. Distinguish openWEPP process ownership from
wepppy forcing/orchestration ownership. Do not treat an unavailable operand as
permission to infer causality from outcome correlation.

### Phase D — Freeze successor admission and promotion rules

Define prospective materiality and noninferiority semantics, component-specific
longwave and sublimation efficacy, combined-interaction rules, held-out
validation, stop-losses, and the exact evidence that admits EB-04V, EB-04W, or
EB-04X. Do not inspect new candidate results in this phase.

### Phase E — Explain, review, verify, and hand off

Produce figures and sidecars, reconcile roadmap/catalog status, run scoped
validation, complete dual independent reviews and finding disposition, obtain
dual terminal verification, and publish the bounded successor handoff.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Exactly 16 immutable EB-04T failures reconstruct into exactly nine density,
   two Harvard geometry/interception, and five open-control under-persistence
   rows with no unclassified or multiply counted row.
2. Every row has a named observation operator, units, phase, candidate owner,
   competing explanation, and mechanism-discriminating evidence requirement.
3. Density operators separate KGE components and seasonal phases; no single
   coefficient is proposed to repair mixed high/low bias.
4. Geometry operators first prove algebraic SWE–density–depth closure and use
   the open lane to anchor non-canopy behavior before interpreting the paired
   forest residual.
5. Under-persistence operators distinguish pre-peak input deficit from
   post-deposition mass loss and premature ablation using independent mass and
   energy operands.
6. Every required operand has units, sign convention, lineage, availability,
   and anti-alias disposition. Missing observability is assigned to a named
   successor boundary rather than silently inferred.
7. Data roles are prospective, nonoverlapping, and explicit by site/year or
   record unit; diagnostic data are not represented as independent validation.
8. Materiality, efficacy, adjacent-process noninferiority, interaction, and
   stop-loss semantics are frozen without deriving thresholds from EB-04S/04T
   result magnitudes.
9. EB-04V/04W/04X admission matrices identify authoritative implementation,
   research, forcing-owner, or bounded model-limit routes without proxy physics.
10. Figures are readable and each has a same-stem Markdown sidecar covering
    question, population, units, method, interpretation, and limitations.
11. No model subprocess, production/test/contract/fixture/observation edit,
    calibration, new candidate result, or default/promotion change occurs.
12. Scoped validation, dual review, finding disposition, dual verification,
    exact-diff, security, line-count, roadmap/catalog, and handoff gates pass.

## Surprises & Discoveries

- Retained Stage 3 fields can reconstruct cold-content/vapor energy, but they
  are not the authoritative CoE melt-energy ledger. CoE melt drivers and
  separately named rain/sensible heat are additional EB-04W gaps.
- A candidate-derived seasonal phase would permit date-membership leakage.
  Primary comparisons therefore use one observed-anchored date frame shared
  by baseline and every candidate; dry/wet/model-peak labels are diagnostic.
- Current authority cannot justify a numeric efficacy replication/site-spread
  threshold. Result-bearing efficacy work remains closed until a successor
  seals those values prospectively from independent authority.
