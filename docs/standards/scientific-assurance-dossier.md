# Scientific Assurance Dossier Standard — V1 Retirement Notice

Status: proposed retirement; frozen under an interim no-new-public-v1-authoring
moratorium pending ASSURE-02 acceptance

The v1 public “scientific assurance dossier” format is not the openWEPP v2
publication standard. Its status-first structure and public candidate posture
failed the intended communication outcome for hydrologists, soil scientists,
researchers, and practitioners.

No new public v1 dossier may be authored, promoted, snapshotted, or vendored
during the acceptance hold. Proposed v2 work follows the
[scientific model-evaluation report standard](scientific-model-evaluation-report.md)
and [v2 architecture](../governance/scientific-assurance-v2-architecture.md) in
nonpublic staging only until the acceptance gate.
The word **dossier** may still describe the complete internal report,
supplement, machine, review, and snapshot bundle. It must not name or format the
primary public scientific product.

The v1 source, generated SNOTEL candidate, schemas, compiler behavior, and
work-package records remain historical evidence until ASSURE-03 removes them
from active/public catalogs and preserves an exact archive. This notice does not
reassess or diminish the underlying snow/frost science, datasets, contracts,
campaigns, or model narrative.

Do not promote, release-snapshot, cite as openWEPP's snow/frost assessment, or
vendor the v1 candidate.

On explicit ASSURE-02 acceptance, the same recorded disposition must atomically
mark ADR-0038 accepted, activate the v2 report standard, and make this v1
retirement final. Until then neither publication architecture is active for new
public assurance content.
