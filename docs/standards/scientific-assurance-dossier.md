# Scientific Assurance Dossier Standard — V1 Retired

Status: retired by accepted ADR-0038 and ASSURE-03

The v1 public “scientific assurance dossier” format is not an openWEPP
publication standard. Its status-first structure and public candidate posture
failed the intended communication outcome for hydrologists, soil scientists,
researchers, and practitioners. No public v1 dossier may be authored, promoted,
snapshotted, restored, cited as an openWEPP scientific assessment, or vendored.

New scientific-assurance work follows the active
[scientific model-evaluation report standard](scientific-model-evaluation-report.md),
[v2 architecture](../governance/scientific-assurance-v2-architecture.md), and
[lifecycle contract](../governance/scientific-assurance-dossier-lifecycle.md).
Drafts and review candidates stay outside public `usersum`; only independently
reviewed, approved, and release-transferred reports are published.

The word **dossier** may still describe the complete internal report,
supplement, machine, review, and snapshot bundle. It must not name or format the
primary public scientific product.

ASSURE-03 binds the exact v1 source, generated SNOTEL candidate, schemas,
compiler, review records, and work-package provenance to frozen Git and SHA-256
identities before removing active/public routes. That historical recovery
record does not reassess or diminish the snow/frost science, datasets,
contracts, campaigns, or model narrative, and it does not authorize public
restoration.
