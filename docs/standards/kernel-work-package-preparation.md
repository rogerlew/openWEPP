# Kernel Work-Package Preparation Procedure

Status: Active. Applies to kernel behavior and runtime projection controlling
kernel branches. The common package guide owns structure and review counts;
this procedure adds scientific acceptance, not extra administrative artifacts.

## Package preparation
Create one package.md at the authorized scope. Do not widen or merge packages to
amortize ceremony. State objective, protected boundaries, affected mechanisms,
authority, acceptance and selected checks before substantive edits.
For DC work retain the correction envelope and seven-gate conversion rule in
../defect_closure_execplans.md; continue any authority-backed in-scope correction.

Read root/common/write-path instructions, then relevant sections of the canonical
SC authority and ../work-packages/science-obligations.md. Read authoring/profile
rules when authoring authority; do not require every role to preload that entire
route. Single-file contracts support section selection under the science-contract
guide. Include affected definitions, units, guards, consumers and cross-cutting
dependencies; uncertainty expands reading. No separate reading map or budget report.

## Contract-first and provenance
1. Amend or confirm canonical SC authority for the intended behavior.
2. Add/update contract-derived tests.
3. Record pre-implementation contract-gate evidence.
4. Implement the production correction, then run applicable validation.

Package notes never replace canonical authority. Every equation, constant, guard
and invariant must trace to that authority. Legacy physics migration targets
/workdir/wepp-forest_260430_baseline at dac3c950d8b16cc73774bf5ce2e7e11f80baac70
unless a canonical decision supersedes it. Preserve legacy symbol continuity or
explicit aliases. Do not implement surrogate/proxy/provisional/heuristic process
physics. Missing or contradictory science authority blocks dependent implementation;
available authoritative science remains an implementation obligation.

Solver work follows numerical-solver-architecture.md: one canonical numerical
solver per pre-iteration physical regime, typed failure or canonical adaptive
response on nonconvergence, and deletion of superseded production paths.
Preserve typed guards; no silent defaults, unbounded clamping or canonicalize-and-
proceed unless a canonical SC explicitly authorizes bounded normalization with
threshold, units, provenance and tests.

## Scientific acceptance
- Retain touched contract invariants and applicable A0/A1/A3 authority gates.
- Real direct-path/activation/publication claims require the actual downstream
  consumer plus negative proof the old compatibility path does not carry them.
- Conservation-sensitive outputs require operand lineage before production edits:
  field, units, normalization/denominator, area/volume basis, source authority,
  and authoritative versus diagnostic classification. Fixtures separate plausible
  wrong aliases/formulas. Independently reconstruct produced outputs and perform
  real closure/magnitude audits; use two-sided bounds when physically supported.
  Self-consistency and one-sided bounds alone cannot close acceptance.
- Preserve metadata/schema alignment and external-authority anti-evasion checks
  when suite posture, cohort fixtures or required-case bindings change.
- Freeze scientific measurement predicates, tolerances and observation roles
  before result-bearing execution. Keep exact relevant source/input/binary
  identity and raw evidence. Reviewers challenge unsupported claims and
  anti-tautology, not the presence of redundant paperwork.

## Parameterized science
Apply ADR-0042 and science-obligations.md. Declare implementation, calibration
readiness, empirical calibration, independent validation or their combination.
Distinguish mathematical validity, evidence-supported bounds and
ASSUMED_FOR_EXECUTION demonstration bounds. Data limitations do not authorize
proxy physics or an implementation HOLD when in-scope authoritative work remains.
Retain applicable deterministic parameter execution, observation operator and
objective reconstruction, sensitivity/identifiability, boundary/failure/
equifinality reporting, and synthetic recovery where structurally meaningful.
Synthetic recovery is not empirical calibration or independent validation.
Record science_implementation_status, calibration_evidence_status and
identifiability_status with every applicable science-contract-spec readiness
obligation, evidence and rationale, in package.md or a necessary machine-readable
matrix. A required unresolved readiness obligation blocks its readiness claim.
Assign data roles CALIBRATION, INDEPENDENT_VALIDATION or DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY
prospectively; calibration data cannot also prove independent validation.

## Execution and closure
Run requirements under testing-and-gate-strategy.md. Critical changes retain
immediate full correctness regression; campaign/release boundaries retain their
qualification. Required current obligations cannot be deferred retrospectively.
Use the common guide's consequence-based independent reviews, with reviewer-owned
fix verification and independent scientific reconstruction. No fresh verifier
wave, placeholder artifacts, duplicate kickoff or line-count report.
Record commands/results, findings/fixes, remaining blockers and disposition in
package.md. A truthful negative experiment can complete its study scope without
claiming production readiness. In-scope correctness work continues until handled.
