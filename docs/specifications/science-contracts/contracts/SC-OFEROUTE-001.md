# SC-OFEROUTE-001 Hillslope OFE-by-OFE Overland-Flow Routing Process Contract

Status: `approved`
Maturity: `active`
Evidence mode: `static`

## Purpose

Define top-down scientific authority for conditional-default hillslope
overland-flow routing that replaces the legacy equivalent-plane / equilibrium-storage
representation with **OFE-by-OFE kinematic-wave routing under space/time-variant
flow resistance** (Papanicolaou et al. 2018). This contract governs the
friction-factor menu, the 1-D kinematic-wave solution and its TVD-MacCormack
shock-capturing scheme, the CFL stability policy, and the per-OFE hydrograph
handoff. It is the canonical authority that the MOFEFID Lane D solver
(`ADR-0033`, ratified in narrowed scope) requires **before** solver
implementation, and it anchors the already-landed shadow-first friction
kernels (`ofe_routing::friction`).

## Scientific Scope

In scope:
- Additive friction menu: skin (grain + raindrop), form (isolated roughness
  elements), wave, and vegetation resistance, and the equivalent
  Darcy-Weisbach `f_eq`.
- 1-D kinematic-wave approximation (continuity + `q = alpha h^m`) solved per
  OFE with the TVD-MacCormack predictor/corrector and dissipative flux limiter.
- CFL sub-timestep policy within the hillslope day loop.
- Per-OFE outlet-hydrograph -> downstream-OFE upstream-boundary handoff over
  the `INV-RUNOFFPART-029` transfer seam.
- Per-OFE Green-Ampt infiltration on rainfall producing the routed excess,
  with active routing SUPERSEDING DC01's daily-lump runon re-infiltration
  (`SC-RUNOFFPART-001#INV-RUNOFFPART-031`); the routed runon is surface-routed,
  not re-infiltrated.
- Conditional default activation posture: active routing is the default only
  when every scheduled lane has complete native routing-coefficient authority;
  coefficient-absent runs stay on the legacy/off path, and mixed authority
  fails closed.
- Subsurface-to-surface **exfiltration coupling** when the subsystem is active:
  subsurface excess (return flow / saturation-excess exfiltration) entering the
  OFE surface routing as a source term, and conservation/export of **baseflow**
  (subsurface outflow not exfiltrated), so an active router closes the full
  hillslope water balance. The subsurface *flow physics* stays `SC-SUBHYD-001`'s;
  the coupling *seam* is an activation requirement here (`GAP-OFEROUTE-006`,
  `INV-OFEROUTE-012`).
- Activation-candidate hourly surface-source shape governance for the Lane D
  shadow and the future active seam: DC01/ADR-0036 source weights may combine
  rainfall excess, subsurface exfiltration/saturation carry, and a
  source-authorized snowmelt/routed-liquid hourly limb owned by
  `SC-RUNOFFPART-001`.

Out of scope:
- Rainfall-excess / infiltration internals owned by `SC-RUNOFFPART-001`.
- Subsurface lateral flow **physics** owned by `SC-SUBHYD-001` (Darcy `latqcc`,
  percolation). NOTE: the **coupling** of subsurface excess into the active
  surface router (return flow / saturation excess) and baseflow accounting is
  IN scope as an activation seam — `GAP-OFEROUTE-006` / `INV-OFEROUTE-012`.
- Watershed **channel** routing owned by `SC-ROUTE-001` (this contract is the
  **hillslope overland** analog, deliberately distinct).
- Sediment detachment/transport owned by `SC-SED-001` (this contract produces
  the hydraulic and routed-hydrograph shape surfaces that sediment coupling
  consumes; it does not own sediment process math).
- Universal default activation regardless of native routing-coefficient
  authority.
- Kernel Rust API naming.

## Algorithm Specification

1. **Friction menu (eqs. 2-7).** Per OFE per sub-timestep compute `f_s`
   (regime-dispatched by `Re`), `f_f`, `f_w` (`Fr`-gated), `f_veg`, and the
   additive `f_eq = f_s + f_f + f_w + f_veg`; derive `C = sqrt(8 g / f_eq)` and
   `alpha = C S_o^0.5`.
2. **KWE (eqs. A1-A2).** Continuity `dh/dt + dq/dx = v` with `q = alpha h^m`,
   `m = 1.5`.
3. **TVD-MacCormack (eqs. 8-14, rev-24 source-corrected form).**
   Predictor/corrector over the OFE mesh; final depth is the
   predictor/corrector average plus the TVD dissipation; final discharge
   `q = alpha h^1.5`. The flux limiter is
   `phi(r) = min(2r, 1) for r > 0; 0 for r <= 0` (Davis eq. 3.20; Mingham
   eq. 31f) — R-63's printed (11c) swaps the branches and is adjudicated a
   transcription error against R-63's own citation chain
   (REF-OFEROUTE-TVD-MACCORMACK). The dissipation is the two-sided
   FACE-BASED form: per interior face `i+1/2`,
   `D_{i+1/2} = [G(r+_i) + G(r-_{i+1})] (h_{i+1} - h_i)` with
   `G(r) = 0.5 Cf(Cr) (1 - phi(r))`, `r+_i = dh_{i-1/2}/dh_{i+1/2}`,
   `r-_i = dh_{i+1/2}/dh_{i-1/2}` (Mingham eqs. 28b/31a/31g; Davis eqs.
   3.17-3.18); each cell receives `D_{i+1/2} - D_{i-1/2}` and the
   domain-boundary faces carry ZERO dissipative flux, so the term
   telescopes exactly (creates/destroys no mass, `INV-OFEROUTE-006`).
   Rev-26 recorded departures (openWEPP-owned, on the Case-4 acceptance
   path): (i) MATERIAL-INTERFACE faces (cell parameters differ across the
   face — slope/roughness breaks) carry zero dissipative flux, because
   the uniform-coefficient Davis/Mingham analysis does not apply there
   and the equilibrium depth jump at such faces is physical, not an
   oscillation; (ii) a boundary-adjacent face whose smoothness-monitor
   stencil leaves the domain (or crosses a break) MIRRORS the available
   side's ratio so boundary oscillations are still detected; a face with
   neither monitor carries no dissipation. Both are mass-neutral (face
   fluxes) and validated by the oracle-convergence and conservation
   gates.
4. **CFL (eq. 12, rev-47 form).** `Cr = (Delta t / Delta x) c <= 1` at
   every cell with `c` the TRUE kinematic celerity `dq/dh` of the
   equilibrium friction law, computed from the implicit relation
   `q = sqrt(8 g S_o / f_eq(q,h)) h^1.5` by differentiating the active
   friction branch:
   `c = q (m/h - 0.5 (partial f_eq / partial h)/f_eq) /
   (1 + 0.5 q (partial f_eq / partial q)/f_eq)`.
   The branch derivatives are taken from the same active friction-menu
   terms used to compute `f_eq`: Manning uses the definitional
   `q = (sqrt(S_o)/n) h^(5/3)` and `c = (5/3) q/h`; the pure laminar
   skin limb gives `c = 3 q/h`; the pure Hirsch turbulent skin limb gives
   `c = (1.5 / (1 - 0.225)) q/h ~= 1.93548 q/h`; additive menus sum the
   active component derivatives (`skin`, `form`, `wave`, `vegetation`)
   before applying the implicit formula. At branch discontinuities
   (`Re ~= 1000`, `Fr ~= 0.5`, or submergence limits), use the derivative
   of the selected active branch; do not smooth, average, or perturb the
   physics branch. If the pure-skin discontinuity leaves no self-consistent
   closed-form root on either side, the selected branch is the pre-step
   discharge branch, again without smoothing. The celerity remains floored
   at the frozen-alpha expression `1.5 alpha h^0.5` (the discrete step's
   own flux speed).
   Rationale (D10B S4 evidence): the frozen-alpha expression under-estimates
   the wave speed for depth-dependent friction (2x on the laminar
   `k_o/Re` limb, whose equilibrium celerity is `3 q/h`; Manning
   `(5/3) q/h`), and the pre-rev-26 scheme ran at true Courant ~1.8 — a
   latent instability masked by the pre-rev-24 inverted limiter's blanket
   dissipation. Rev 47 replaces the finite-difference perturbed-depth
   celerity evaluation with the analytic derivative above; it does not
   change the CFL target, TVD scheme, closure tolerances, or friction law.
   `alpha` remains frozen through the step (Tseng 2010 family precedent;
   the alpha lag is first-order-in-time and adjudicated by the oracle
   convergence harness).
5. **Boundary fluxes and mass ledger (rev 24).** The upstream boundary
   condition `q_up` must enter the discrete scheme so that the mass
   actually injected per step equals the booked `q_up dt`; the downstream
   outflow closure must be upwind-consistent (kinematic information
   travels downstream only) with the booked outflow equal to the scheme's
   actual boundary flux. The conservation ledger books the SCHEME's actual
   boundary fluxes — a ledger that books different quadratures than the
   scheme moves is non-compliant (D10B S0 ledger evidence: booking
   mismatches of 16-28% of source masqueraded as "conservation residual").
   Rev 41 positivity preservation: before predictor/corrector stage depths are
   formed, the stage face fluxes are capped by the upwind cell's available
   water over the substep (current storage + valid incoming face flux + valid
   source), using the same conservative stage-face representation that books
   the downstream boundary flux. The final TVD face correction is scaled
   uniformly toward zero only when applying it at full strength would make any
   cell negative; because the TVD term is face-based and telescoping, this
   scaling preserves conservation. Residual `h.max(0)` clamp booking is
   retained only for sub-roundoff dry-floor cleanup and remains surfaced.
6. **Per-OFE handoff (rev-26 form).** The OFE outlet hydrograph becomes
   the downstream OFE's upstream boundary condition, carried over the
   `INV-RUNOFFPART-029` seam as an hourly profile (not a daily
   aggregate). The transfer substrate is the CONSERVATIVE PER-BIN
   OUTFLOW SERIES (piecewise-constant per sample bin, carrying the
   scheme's ACTUAL discharged mass; the exported hydrograph is the
   bin-mean boundary flux): the downstream solver's per-step injection
   integrates this series exactly (span-aware for a partial final bin,
   so no mass is stranded when the window is not a multiple of the bin
   width), and seam volumes are booked from solver ledgers, not from
   sample-grid quadratures. Transient negative per-bin boundary
   attribution (dry-outlet front arrival) is redistributed forward to a
   non-negative series with the exact total preserved; the downstream
   validity guard fails closed on any residual negative injection.
