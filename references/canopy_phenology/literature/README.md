# Canopy Phenology Literature

These source-native files support or audit claims in William J. Elliot’s April
2026 report. Operator-supplied PDFs and Markdown were moved from `~/Downloads`
on 2026-07-26. Dun et al. (2009) and Srivastava et al. (2020) were downloaded
from their official USDA Forest Service Treesearch records.

The Hubbard Brook synthesis-book files were copied from
<https://github.com/hbr-lter/synthesisbook/> at commit
`3bb5b43e1429172b8d002e4b002d6a31db694ad1`. The upstream repository did not
contain an explicit `LICENSE` file at that commit; retain the source identity
and do not infer a license.

Important audit notes:

- Yang et al. (2018) Table 2 labels `7.6 Mg ha-1` as standing foliage biomass,
  not annual leaf fall.
- Coates et al. (2020) reports forest-floor mass separately from total fuel
  mass and reports hardwood/softwood percentages as basal-area composition, not
  fuel composition.
- Gresham (1982) reports total litterfall containing foliage plus woody and
  reproductive material at a nearby coastal South Carolina site, not Santee
  leaf litter alone.
- Amatya and Trettin (2019) reports watershed runoff as a fraction of
  precipitation; it is not a hillslope surface-runoff target.
